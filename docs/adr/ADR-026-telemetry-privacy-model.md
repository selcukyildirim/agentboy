# ADR-026: Product Telemetry Privacy Model

## Status

Proposed

## Context

Privacy-preserving product analytics.

## Decision

Anonymous, opt-in telemetry with business content exclusion.

## Alternatives

- No telemetry: Less visibility
- Full tracking: Privacy concerns

## Consequences

- User opt-in required
- No business content in telemetry
- Aggregate metrics only
- Easy opt-out
